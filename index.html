How Booking Works

1. Choose

Select your car and rental plan.

2. Book

Pay ₹500 booking advance and complete verification.

3. Drive

Pay the remaining amount and security deposit, inspect the car and start your journey.

Rental Rules & Requirements

  • Minimum booking: 3 hours.
  • Booking advance: ₹500; refund depends on the situation.
  • Remaining payment before taking the car.
  • Security deposit: ₹10,000; refunded after inspection.
  • Fuel: full-to-full or same level at which the car was given.
  • Low fuel charge: ₹400 per point.
  • Extra fuel returned may be refunded after inspection.
  • More than 30 minutes late: 1 hour is countable.
  • Late return charge: ₹300/hour.
  • Early return does not reduce the minimum 3-hour charge.
  • Outstation and other-state trips allowed.
  • Nepal allowed only with prior confirmation and required information.
  • Anyone with a valid driving licence can drive.
  • Minimum customer age: 18 years.
  • Aadhaar in any valid format accepted.
  • Mobile verification and emergency contact required.
  • Photo/video verification required.
  • Smoking inside the car prohibited.
  • Pets prohibited.
  • Alcohol/drinking inside the car prohibited.
  • Commercial/taxi use prohibited.
  • Racing/rash driving prohibited.
  • Re-renting/subletting prohibited.
  • Off-road/rough-terrain use prohibited.
  • Damage/accident handled according to agreed rental terms and applicable requirements.
  • Payment: UPI, cash and bank transfer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take the car outstation?

Yes. Outstation and other-state trips are allowed. Nepal requires prior confirmation.

What is the security deposit?

₹10,000 for either car, refundable after inspection subject to the rental terms.

How much booking advance is required?

₹500.

What documents are required?

Valid driving licence, Aadhaar, mobile verification, emergency contact and photo/video verification.

What happens if I return late?

More than 30 minutes late counts as 1 hour, with a ₹300/hour late-return charge.
